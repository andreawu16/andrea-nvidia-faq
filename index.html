FAQ

Common questions

What's the general hiring process?
The process typically includes resume screening, a few rounds of technical interviews (coding test, system design, or role-specific assessment), and a final interview with the hiring manager. End-to-end it usually takes 2–4 weeks.
What should I prepare for the interview?
It depends on the role. Software engineers should expect coding and system design rounds. Hardware roles often involve domain knowledge deep-dives. I'll brief you on what to expect once we connect.
Can candidates outside of Taiwan apply?
Most of our open roles are targeting Taiwan-based talent, but I'm always happy to connect if you're open to relocating. Relocation support and work visa assistance are available for eligible roles.
What positions are currently open?
We're actively hiring across AI, GPU, software, and hardware. Visit the NVIDIA Careers page for the latest listings, or reach out to me directly and I'll share what's most relevant to your background.
Will you refer me internally?
If your profile aligns with our open roles, I'll flag it to our internal hiring teams and help make your application visible. Send me your resume with a short intro about your experience and interests.
How do I submit my resume?
Email me at andrewu@nvidia.com with your resume and a brief note about your background and target role. The more specific you are, the faster I can match you.
